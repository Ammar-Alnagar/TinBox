## Exercise 3: Measurement & Reasoning Exercise
- Question: An inference engine serving layer handles 50 concurrent client connections over Server-Sent Events (SSE). Each client receives an average of 40 tokens per second. Each SSE event frame contains approximately 120 bytes of JSON payload.
  1. What is the total outgoing network throughput generated by the serving layer in KB/s?
  2. Over a 10-minute serving period, how many megabytes of network data are transmitted?
- Calculation:
  $$\text{Tokens per second across all streams} = 50 \times 40 = 2,000 \text{ tokens/s}$$
  $$\text{Throughput} = 2,000 \times 120 \text{ bytes/s} = 240,000 \text{ bytes/s} \approx 234.375 \text{ KB/s}$$
  $$\text{Total Time} = 10 \times 60 = 600 \text{ seconds}$$
  $$\text{Total Data} = 240,000 \times 600 = 144,000,000 \text{ bytes} \approx 137.33 \text{ MB}$$
- Answer: **234.38 KB/s** throughput and **137.33 MB** transmitted over 10 minutes.
